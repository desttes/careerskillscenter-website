Skilled Trades

Build a Hands-On Career

The trades are hiring. Electricians, HVAC technicians and plumbers are retiring faster than they are being replaced, and contractors across Massachusetts are competing for trained help. Our trade programs put tools in your hands early and prepare you for the credentials employers and licensing boards ask for.

  • Shop and lab time, not just lecture
  • Safety credentials employers expect
  • Apprenticeship and licensure preparation
  • Day and evening options

Electrical Technician

Wiring, circuits, load calculations, and safe installation practice for residential and light commercial work.

Credential: OSHA 10 · apprenticeship prep Length: TBD

HVAC/R Technician

Heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration service, diagnostics and installation.

Credential: EPA Section 608 · OSHA 10 Length: TBD

Plumbing Technician

Pipefitting, fixture installation, venting, drainage and water supply systems.

Credential: OSHA 10 · apprenticeship prep Length: TBD

Welding

SMAW, MIG and TIG fundamentals with booth time, blueprint reading and weld inspection basics.

Credential: AWS entry-level welder prep Length: TBD

Carpentry & Construction

Framing, finish carpentry, blueprint reading, materials and jobsite safety.

Credential: OSHA 10 · NCCER Core prep Length: TBD

Information Technology

Launch Your Tech Career

You do not need a four-year degree to work in IT. Employers hire on certifications and demonstrated skill. Our IT track moves from the help desk fundamentals every employer tests for up through networking, security and cloud, so you can stack credentials as you go.

  • Hands-on labs and real hardware
  • Industry certification exam preparation
  • Stackable credentials you can build on
  • Resume and interview coaching included

IT Support Specialist

Hardware, operating systems, troubleshooting and ticketing — the core help desk skill set.

Credential: CompTIA A+ prep Length: TBD

Network Technician

Routing, switching, cabling, wireless and network troubleshooting.

Credential: CompTIA Network+ prep Length: TBD

Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Threats, access control, encryption basics and incident response fundamentals.

Credential: CompTIA Security+ prep Length: TBD

Cloud Fundamentals

Core cloud concepts, services, billing and security across major providers.

Credential: AWS Cloud Practitioner or AZ-900 prep Length: TBD

Medical

Care for Your Community

Massachusetts is one of the largest healthcare employment markets in the country. Clinics, hospitals and long-term care facilities across the state need trained support staff now. These programs prepare you for entry-level clinical and administrative roles and the certification exams that go with them.

  • Clinical skills practice in a lab setting
  • Certification exam preparation
  • Externship placement support
  • Pathways into further nursing and allied health study

Medical Assistant

Clinical and administrative duties: vitals, patient intake, injections, scheduling and records.

Credential: CCMA prep Length: TBD

Phlebotomy Technician

Venipuncture technique, specimen handling, safety and patient care.

Credential: CPT prep Length: TBD

EKG Technician

Electrocardiogram setup, lead placement, rhythm recognition and reporting.

Credential: CET prep Length: TBD

Certified Nursing Assistant

Direct patient care, mobility, hygiene, vitals and documentation.

Credential: MA Nurse Aide Registry prep Length: TBD

Medical Billing & Coding
