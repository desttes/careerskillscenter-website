## Facts to add
- **What an ITA can cover (Massachusetts):** tuition, and often books, fees and certification exams, up to the career center's limit. (Project doc 02, from MA ETPL/ITA materials) `[VERIFY with a career center page before publishing]`
- **What it usually doesn't:** living expenses, and costs above the cap. Training that starts before approval.
- **Help that may be available separately:** "supportive services" (for example, transportation or childcare help) may be offered by some centers. Say "ask your counselor" `[VERIFY]`.
- **Income while training:** Section 30/TOP keeps unemployment benefits going during full-time approved training (up to 26 extra weeks; apply by the 20th paid week). This answers the #1 real-world blocker.
- **Caps differ by region**, which is why we don't state a number.
- **Honest comparison box:** "Free to you" vs. "free with conditions": you need to be eligible, attend, complete, and many centers track whether you get a job afterward.
