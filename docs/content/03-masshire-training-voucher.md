## Step-by-step (use numbered H2s; say "steps vary a little by center")
1. **Create a MyMassGov account and register in MassHire JobQuest** (jobquest.mass.gov). Save your Jobseeker ID. (masshiredowntownboston.org; masshiremncareers.com)
2. **Find your MassHire Career Center.** It's based on where you live or last worked. Link to the mass.gov locator `[VERIFY URL]`.
3. **Attend the Training Information Meeting.** Some centers hold it in person; some (e.g., Downtown Boston) require a training-grants video, then a screening form.
4. **Take the TABE assessment** (reading and math) if your center requires it.
5. **Meet a career counselor** and build a career plan. Some centers require a signed engagement letter (Metro North).
6. **Gather documents** (list in README).
7. **Pick an approved program.** It must be on the ETPL; search it in JobQuest's "Locate Training." Bring the school's program details, cost and schedule.
8. **Submit the ITA / training proposal.** The center reviews it and approves (or not) based on eligibility, job demand, and available funds.
9. **Wait for approval before you start class.** Training that starts before approval usually can't be paid for.
- **Timeline callout:** about 6–8 weeks (MassHire Central).
- **Tip box:** "On unemployment? Ask about Section 30 at the same time" → link to post 1.
- **Tip box:** "Funds run out. Start early in the program year."
