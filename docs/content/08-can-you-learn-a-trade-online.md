## Honest core message
Online teaches theory, code, safety and math, and it prepares you for exams. **Massachusetts trade licenses require supervised on-the-job hours**, which can't be done online. Best path: online theory + apprenticeship/on-the-job hours.

## Facts to add (Massachusetts)
- Electricians, plumbers, gasfitters, sheet metal workers and refrigeration technicians are **licensed by the state** (Division of Occupational Licensure boards). HVAC isn't one license; it spans refrigeration technician, sheet metal and gasfitter licenses. (Project doc 02) `[VERIFY board names and hour requirements at mass.gov before stating any hour numbers]`
- **EPA 608** certification (federal) is required to handle refrigerants, and the exam prep can be done online.
- **OSHA 10/30** safety training is commonly available online.
- **Registered Apprenticeship** in MA (Division of Apprentice Standards): earn while you learn; classroom hours are required and can often be online. (Project doc 11)
