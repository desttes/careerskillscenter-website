## Facts to add
- **Basic requirements most centers list:** 18+ for Adult/Dislocated Worker funds; live or have worked in that center's service area; authorized to work in the US; men born after Jan 1, 1960 registered with Selective Service. (Source: masshirecentralcc.com training-assistance page)
- **Three WIOA groups, explained simply:**
  - *Dislocated worker*: laid off or terminated (or notified), eligible for or exhausted unemployment, unlikely to return to the same industry; also includes some displaced homemakers and formerly self-employed people. (WIOA §3 definitions, U.S. DOL)
  - *Adult*: 18+. Priority goes to people receiving public assistance, other low-income people, and people with basic-skills needs. (WIOA priority of service, U.S. DOL)
  - *Youth*: roughly ages 14–24, with different rules. Mention briefly; not our audience.
- **Veterans and eligible spouses get priority.**
- **Income example:** MassHire Central's 2026 thresholds run from **$15,960 to $60,124+** by family size. Say clearly that each region publishes its own table and the reader should ask their center.
