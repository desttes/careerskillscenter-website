# Editorial decisions: How to Become a CNA in Massachusetts

Rule applied: approved only if corroborated (2+ people in separate threads, or an official source), no CONTRADICTED claims, strategist KEEP (MAYBE only if it adds balance), current and U.S.-applicable. Stricter call wins. Unsure = cut.

Limits: max 3 quotes (C1, C4, C7). Attribute as "CNAs say…". No usernames, no links. Never imply they are CSC students. No pay rates, bonuses, costs or "3 days" timing from posts. Date exam tips "as of May 2026" (revised state exam expected early 2027).

## APPROVED

**C1 – Skills test (exam day box + new "Why people fail the skills test" section). QUOTE + verified facts.**
- Quote: "How you do stuff at work and how you do stuff when taking the state exam are two different things."
- CHANGE: the "say every step out loud" tip is CONTRADICTED (handbook: steps only verbalized do not count). Drop it. Say instead: do each step, don't just talk through it; only a few steps need words (e.g. soak times, telling the observer you are finished).
- Verified to use (D&S May 2026 handbook v7.0): do all bold critical steps and 80% of the other steps on each task; steps are not order-dependent unless marked BEFORE/AFTER; results screen has "View Failed Steps"; Test Review: call D&S first, $25 refundable deposit, within 3 business days. Do not say hand washing is the first step.
- Mix: all experiences agree; no balance needed.

**C2 – "Free" training and contracts (in "3 ways to pay"; questions go in the questions box). PARAPHRASE.**
- Show both sides: some people owed nothing; others signed 1–2 year work contracts with payback threats.
- Do NOT say payback clauses are unenforceable or rarely enforced. Do NOT say you can't be asked to pay back before 12 months (that 12 months is the state's reimbursement window).
- Verified: 42 CFR 483.152(c) – a nursing facility can't charge an aide employed or offered a job when training starts; State must reimburse pro rata if a facility hires a self-paying trainee within 12 months.
- Wording: ask in writing what happens if you leave; call DPH or free legal aid before signing.

**C3 – Choosing a first workplace (new "Before you take the job" checklist). PARAPHRASE.**
- No quote (lowercase quote can't be edited).
- Drop the "below 3 stars can't host clinicals" claim (CONTRADICTED; rule is about enforcement history). Say only: Medicare Care Compare shows separate ratings for health inspections, staffing and quality. No residents-per-aide number.
- Suggest asking how many residents each aide has, without stating a standard.

**C4 – First weeks feel overwhelming (checklist/"is this job for you"). QUOTE.**
- Quote: "You will be slow at first and that's okay." Pair with "slow is normal, unsafe is not."
- Mix: also say that some units and shifts are harder; it's okay to leave an unsafe workplace.

**C5 – Back injuries (next to the BLS injury line). PARAPHRASE.**
