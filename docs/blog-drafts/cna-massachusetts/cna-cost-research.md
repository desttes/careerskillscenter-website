# CNA program cost research (Massachusetts)

Date seen: 2026-10-04 (all pages below fetched this date unless marked). Prices change by cohort; re-check before publishing.
Method: WebFetch of each public program page = OPENED. Facts only seen in a web search summary = SEARCH SUMMARY. Nothing estimated.
Note: WebFetch summarizes pages with a small model, so wording of "includes" lists is the tool's paraphrase of the page. Spot-check the top 2-3 pages by eye before quoting.

## Bottom line for the post

- OPENED, public-price programs (7 paid, quoted prices): $1,395 tuition (Lunder, about $1,700 with itemized fees) up to $3,000 (Greenfield CC, not currently offered). Most community-college list prices cluster at $1,550 to $1,850.
- Defensible plain-language range: "about $1,400 to $3,000, with most community-college programs near $1,500 to $1,900." Do not write "$500 to $1,000": no opened page showed that.
- Lowest OPENED price: $1,395 tuition (Lunder CareForce Institute; self-pay total about $1,700 once listed fees are added). Lowest all-in OPENED: MassBay $1,600 (includes scrubs, books, CPR card, lab fee) or Quincy $1,550 + $110 exam + scrubs.
- Highest OPENED price: $3,000 (Greenfield CC, 100 hrs incl. HHA, state test fee included; page says "Not currently offered"). Highest currently listed: $2,880 (Holyoke CC, for people not on benefits) and $2,648 (STCC "CNA Plus", 150 hrs).
- Higher price usually = bundled extras (HHA certificate, dementia care, CPR/first aid, exam fee, more hours). Compare what is included.
- Free routes exist (see bottom). Cost is often $0 for eligible people.
- SEARCH SUMMARY only (do not publish as fact): Bunker Hill CC "6-week, $1,000"; Roxbury CC "$1,500, books not included"; Red Cross Pioneer Valley "from $675".

## Free and funded routes confirmable from a page I opened

1. Cambridge Community Learning Center: fully free, includes exam fee and scrubs; limited eligibility. https://www.cambridgema.gov/Services/cnatrainingprogram (OPENED)
2. Holyoke CC Jump Start: free if receiving SNAP, TAFDC or similar benefits. https://www.hcc.edu/courses-and-programs/workforce-development/free-job-training-programs/jump-start/certified-nursing-assistant (OPENED)
3. Bristol CC: page says price "may be covered by SNAP E&T benefits." (OPENED, bristolcc coursestorm link above)
4. Lunder CareForce Institute: "no cost to students" when employer- or grant-sponsored. (OPENED, URL in row 6)
5. MassBay and Greenfield: scholarships "may be available" for eligible students/cohorts (OPENED pages; no amounts).
6. Employer-run (nursing home) programs, from search summaries, not opened, shown as examples only [SEARCH SUMMARY]:
   - St. Patrick's Manor, Framingham: free, paid about $15/hr during training, must sign 2-year full-time work commitment or repay class cost.
   - Springside Rehab and Nursing Center, Pittsfield: free skills and clinical training, then hired.
   - Timberlyn Heights, Great Barrington: OPENED a job posting: 4-week program, "competitive pay during training," employment as CNA upon completion; cost to student not stated on posting.
   Note for writer: free-if-hired programs may carry a work commitment or repayment clause. Say "ask about the commitment".

## Cautions for the writer

- Prices are per cohort and change; say "as of October 2026" and link to the programs.
- Compare what's included: exam fee ($110 at Quincy and Lunder; state test fee shown as $110 in two programs, which is consistent, but I did not find an official DPH/Red Cross NACE fee page; leave exam-fee citing to the existing brief), uniform, books, CPR, background check (CORI).
- Do not use tradecareerpath, registerednursing.org, dreambound or similar sites; they are not allowed sources and showed odd figures (for example $6,168-$14,899 for BHCC, which is credit-college tuition, not the CNA course).
- Some lengths differ widely (5 to 8 weeks; 100 to 150 hours). Minimum hours rule per DPH page was not read (403). Use the existing research brief for that.
