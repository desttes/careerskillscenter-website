### PTCB: Certified Pharmacy Technician (CPhT) (source: PTCB, via search summary)
- Managing body: Pharmacy Technician Certification Board (PTCB).
- Eligibility: high school diploma or equivalent, plus ONE of: (1) completing a PTCB-Recognized Education/Training Program, or (2) at least 500 hours of work experience as a pharmacy technician. Also subject to PTCB's conduct/licensure-status rules [VERIFY].
- Exam (PTCE): computer-based, **90 multiple-choice questions** (80 scored, 10 unscored), **1 hour 50 minutes**. Scaled score range 1,000 to 1,600; **passing score 1,400**.
- Exam fee: **$129**.
- Renewal: every **2 years**; **20 hours of continuing education** (CE) and a **$49** recertification fee. [VERIFY]
- Do employers require it? Per BLS/CareerOneStop: some states and employers require certification, and even where it is not required it may make it easier to get a job. In Massachusetts it is one of the Board-accepted routes to licensure.

### NHA: ExCPT (Certified Pharmacy Technician) (source: NHA, via search summary)
- Managing body: National Healthcareer Association (NHA).
- Eligibility: high school diploma or GED.
- Exam format: **120 questions** (100 scored, 20 unscored), **130 minutes**; passing scaled score **390** on a 200 to 500 scale.
- Exam fee: **$117** per a search summary (other sources cited $125 to $129, so the fee is unconfirmed). [VERIFY at nhanow.com]
- Retest wait: 60 days minimum, full fee again. [VERIFY]
- Renewal: every 2 years; **$55** fee; **20 hours** of CE. [VERIFY]
