### ASCP Board of Certification: PBT(ASCP) Phlebotomy Technician
- Eligibility (must meet one route; high school diploma or equivalent required for training routes):
  - Route 1: HS diploma or equivalent + completed a NAACLS-approved phlebotomy program within the last 5 years.
  - Route 2: HS diploma or equivalent + completed a structured phlebotomy program within the last 5 years (extract said 40 hours classroom + 100 hours clinical; verify).
  - Another route: 1 year of full-time acceptable clinical experience as a phlebotomy technician within the last 5 years (verify route number, "Route 3" per ASCP form).
  - Another route: valid MLS(ASCP) or MLT(ASCP) certification.
- Exam: 80 multiple-choice questions, 2 hours, computer adaptive testing (CAT).
- Content areas: Circulatory System; Specimen Collection; Specimen Handling, Transport and Processing; Waived and Point-of-Care Testing; Non-Blood Specimens; Laboratory Operations.
- Passing score: scaled score of 400 on a 100-999 scale.
- Cost: application fee $155 per ASCP extract. [VERIFY: a non-official site said $165 after a Jan 2026 increase; confirm on https://apps.ascp.org/services/boc/BOC_Cert_RouteRequirement or the PBT page]
- Renewal: 3-year cycle through the Credential Maintenance Program (CMP). PBT needs 9 points total, including 2 points in phlebotomy and 1 point in laboratory or patient safety. Credential lapses if CMP is not completed.
- Sources: https://www.ascp.org/boc/explore-credentials/view-all-credentials/PBT ; https://www.ascp.org/boc/earn-a-credential/examination-process/about-the-examination ; https://apps.ascp.org/services/boc/BOC_Cert_RouteRequirement ; https://www.ascp.org/boc/maintain-your-credentials/view-credentials-to-maintain/PBT ; https://www.ascp.org/content/docs/default-source/boc-pdfs/boc-us-guidelines/pbt_ipbt_content_guideline.pdf

### NHA: Certified Phlebotomy Technician (CPT)
- Eligibility: HS diploma/GED (or scheduled to earn within 18 months) AND either a phlebotomy training program completed within 5 years, OR 1 year supervised phlebotomy work experience within 3 years (or 2 years within 5 years). Must show at least 30 venipunctures and 10 capillary/finger sticks on live individuals.
- Exam: 120 items (100 scored + 20 unscored pretest), 2 hours.
- Domains (2025 test plan): Safety and Compliance 26 items; Patient Preparation 20; Routine Blood Collections 28; Special Collections 12; Processing 14.

## Training Paths (general industry)
- Typical entry (BLS): postsecondary nondegree award (certificate) from a phlebotomy program at a community college, vocational school or technical school. Programs usually take less than 1 year. Instruction covers anatomy, physiology, medical terminology and lab work, with hands-on blood draw practice.
- Some people qualify with a HS diploma and on-the-job training (BLS).
- Programs may ask for immunization proof, CPR certification, and a background check (from search extract of BLS/ASCP summary; verify against BLS "How to become" text).
- Certification route: finish training, document live sticks (NHA 30 venipunctures + 10 capillary sticks), then pass a national exam.
- Common credential-eligibility training benchmarks from certifying bodies: NAACLS-approved program (ASCP Route 1).
