# Healthcare jobs post: sourced training length and cost facts

Researched 2026-10-04 for `blog/healthcare-jobs-massachusetts` (tools/build-pages.py, `_hcj_faq` and the six-job table).
Legend: **OPENED** = I fetched the page and read its content. **SEARCH SUMMARY** = only seen in a search-result summary, not opened (do not publish without opening). **NOT FOUND** = nothing sourceable; do not estimate.
Dates in "page date" are the "last modified" date the page reported, or the date I accessed it if none.

## Bottom line

- No neutral source (BLS, certifying bodies) gives a typical dollar cost of training. Costs can only be given as **named, dated, public programs** (price page + date) or as **exam fees** from the certifying body. Do not publish a generic "$X to $Y" range.
- BLS gives training LENGTH only in broad terms ("less than 1 year", "1 or 2 years").
- Massachusetts community colleges are free for eligible residents (MassEducate / MassReconnect), which changes the cost story for credit programs. Noncredit workforce programs (Quincy College, STCC) are priced separately.
- Every old figure in the draft table for these six roles is unsupported. Some are contradicted by public programs (see "Old draft figures vs. evidence" at the end).

## 6. Cross-cutting: free community college in Massachusetts

- MassReconnect: free tuition and fees at MA public community colleges for age 25+ without a prior degree. MassEducate (FY2025 budget) extended free tuition/fees to all ages and incomes. Book/supply allowance up to $1,200 by income (and another up to $1,200 for other costs).
- Source: https://www.mass.gov/news/new-data-shows-40-percent-increase-in-students-attending-community-college-through-massreconnect-in-second-year and https://www.mass.gov/info-details/college-comunitarios-gratuitos (search result; not opened). SEARCH SUMMARY. Confirm on the main mass.gov free community college page before publishing. The college page snippets (Middlesex, NECC) repeat the same claim and NECC was OPENED.
- Applies to credit programs. Noncredit workforce programs (Quincy College, STCC WDC) are priced as listed above.

## Suggested safe wording options (for the editor)

- Length: use BLS wording per role ("usually less than 1 year" for phlebotomy, EKG, pharmacy tech on-the-job; "about 1 or 2 years" for medical assistant per BLS), then name one or two dated public programs as examples.
- Cost: "Examples: Quincy College Pharmacy Technician, $1,450 (as of Oct 2026)" and exam fees (PTCB $129, AAPC CPC $425 opened). Add that MA community-college credit programs can be free under MassEducate/MassReconnect.
- Where a role has no sourced cost (EKG tech, medical assistant at Quincy), say "ask the school for the full price".
