# Keyword Analysis — medical assistant and medical billing/coding

Source: Emilio's keyword screenshots (Google Keyword Planner, 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-05; Ahrefs keyword ideas; a question tree for "medical coding" and one for "medical assistant"). Analysis by Claude Code, 2026-10-05. The raw screenshots are on Emilio's computer (`~/Desktop/SEO/`), not in the repo.

## How to read the data
- Keyword Planner volumes are rounded to 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 and are national. Treat them as tiers, not counts.
- "Competition: Low" in Keyword Planner is **paid-ads** competition, not ranking difficulty. The Ahrefs "KD" (Easy / Medium / Hard) is the ranking signal, and "Easy" still needs links for a new site.
- No Massachusetts-modified volumes yet. Pull "medical assistant massachusetts" and similar terms before relying on local numbers.

## Medical assistant (decided: write first; queue topic target keyword "medical assistant massachusetts")
- Universe: 32,818 phrase-match keywords (medical coding: 15,943; coding and billing: 765).
- **Easy, over 10,000 a month (Ahrefs):** medical assistant jobs, salary, jobs near me, programs. Remote medical assistant jobs: Easy, over 1,000. What is a medical assistant: Medium, over 1,000.
- **Hard (skip as targets):** "medical assistant" (head term), "medical assistant certification."
- **Training/course terms** (about 50,000 in Keyword Planner: training, certified program, classes, course, accredited programs): dominated by schools selling courses. Do not target.
- **Certification terms** (about 5,000 each): cma medical, aama certification, amca certification, american association of medical assistants. Point readers to the certifying bodies.
- **Specialties** (about 5,000 each): dermatology, ophthalmic, ob-gyn, pediatric medical assistant. Good sections or a follow-up post.
- **Local/hiring** (about 5,000): hiring, hiring near me, part time.
- **Wrong intent, ignore:** anesthesia assistant, physician assistants, emergency medical assistant, lab/laboratory assistant, pathologist's assistant.
- **Question tree:** MA vs CNA (who gets paid more, what can an MA do that a CNA cannot, which is harder to get, do CNAs draw blood); hard to pass; worth it; why people quit; hard to get hired; will AI replace MAs; how fast can I be an MA; how long to train; typical and highest pay.
- **Angle:** honest career guide with an MA vs CNA section; hiring reality for beginners; CMA vs RMA and whether certification is required (AAMA CMA generally needs an accredited program); national pay figure (national credential).
- **Follow-up posts, in order:** medical assistant salary; MA vs CNA; CMA vs RMA; remote medical assistant jobs.

## Medical assistant, round 2: "how to become" data (Emilio's screenshots, 2026-10-05)
Source: Keyword Planner export (Sept 2025 to Aug 2026, filtered to Low ad competition), Ahrefs "how to become a medical assistant" ideas (109 keywords), and the medical assistant question tree. Analysis by Claude Code. This round **supersedes the "massachusetts" target keyword above.**

**1. Massachusetts is not the keyword. Write a nationwide post (confirmed by data).**
- Ahrefs: "how to become a medical assistant in massachusetts" is under 100 searches a month (Easy). Florida, California, NY, Texas and Arizona are only a little higher (over 100, Easy). Every state version is tiny.
- The national phrase "how to become a medical assistant" is over 1,000 (Hard). The state phrases add up to far less than the national one, and MA is a national credential. Massachusetts becomes one labeled section, not the frame.

**2. Primary keyword and the cluster that fits one post**
- **Primary: "how to become a medical assistant"** (over 1,000, Hard; Hard means a new site needs a clearly better page, not links alone).
- **Fits the same post (all low-volume, high-intent, Easy to moderate):**
  - "medical assistant how many years" (about 500, up 900% in 3 months) = how long training takes.
  - "how hard is it to become a medical assistant" (about 500, up 900% year over year) = the honest-difficulty angle.
  - "how long does it take to train as an MA" and "how fast can I be a medical assistant" (question tree).
  - "is becoming a CMA worth it" / "is becoming an MA worth it" (question tree).
  - "medical assistant salary" (Easy, over 10,000): give pay in the post; a separate salary post is a follow-up.
  - "medical assistant certification" (Hard, over 10,000), "cma medical", "aama certification", "amca certification" (about 5,000; up 900% year over year), "american association of medical assistants", "american medical technologists amt" (about 5,000 each): cover CMA (AAMA), RMA (AMT), CCMA (NHA), and name AMCA as a smaller option. Point to the certifying bodies.
  - "medical assistant part time" (about 5,000).
- **Separate posts, not this one:** MA vs CNA (the question tree's biggest branch: who gets paid more, what can an MA do that a CNA cannot, which is harder to get, do CNAs draw blood); medical assistant salary; remote medical assistant jobs; CMA vs RMA; "medical assistant hiring/jobs near me" (local hiring, about 5,000).

**3. Wrong intent, do not target**
- "how to become a doctor's assistant" shows 50,000, but the likely searcher means **physician assistant**, a master's-degree career. Same for "physician assistants," "medical physician assistant," "surgical/surgery physician assistant," "emergency medicine physician assistant," "anesthesia(logist) assistant," "pathologists assistant programs." Treat these as false demand. One plain sentence in the post saying a medical assistant is not a physician assistant (and what each needs) may catch confused readers; do not chase the volume.
- "medical assistant week" and "medical assistant day" are events (week/day observances), not job seekers.
- "indeed medical assistant" (down 90% year over year) is a job-board brand search.
- Lab/laboratory assistant, clinical lab assistant: different career (about 500 to 5,000).

**4. Adjacent roles that readers confuse with MA** (about 5,000 each: medical office assistant, health care assistant, patient care assistant, clinical assistant; medical administrative/admin 5,000). A short "MA vs medical office assistant vs patient care assistant" clarification serves them. Note that "medical administrative" has the highest ad bids in the export ($7 to $31 a click), so that front-office path has real commercial value, but we sell nothing, so it only signals demand.

**5. Specialty terms** (about 5,000 each): dermatology, ophthalmic, ob-gyn, pediatric medical assistant. A short "where MAs work" section; a follow-up post later.

**Question tree for medical assistant (what readers ask):**
- Branch "who gets paid more, MA or CNA": do CNAs draw blood; what can an MA do that a CNA cannot; which is harder to get; which CNA jobs pay more; what can an RN do that an MA cannot; can you make $100,000 as a CNA.
- Branch "is medical assistant hard to pass": why people quit medical assisting; EMT vs MA pay; typical MA salary; why is it hard to get an MA job; **will AI replace medical assistants**; how fast can I be an MA; is a CMA worth it.
- Branch "what does a medical assistant do": is MA worth it; is an MA higher than a CNA; how long to train; highest pay for a medical assistant; hardest part of the job; how much MAs pay.

**What this means for the builder (decisions stay with the pipeline):** the title, angle and outline are the strategist's call. The data says the winning gap is the **honest beginner questions** (how long, how hard, is it worth it, will AI replace it, is certification required) rather than school lists. SERP competitors are course sellers for the training terms.

## Medical billing and coding (second)
- Salary terms are the biggest Easy cluster (medical coding salary about 50,000 in Keyword Planner; medical billing salary about 5,000).
- Certification terms (CPC, CCS, AAPC) are Hard. Training and class terms are Hard and course-seller territory.
- CPT code terms (cpt codes, 99214, 77063) are people looking up billing codes, not job seekers. Ignore.
- Question tree fits the honest angle: worth it in 2026, AI replacing coders, hard to get hired, remote jobs, billing vs coding pay.
- Remote medical coding jobs: Easy, over 1,000.
- Working title idea: "Is Medical Billing and Coding Worth It? Pay, Training and the AI Question." Link to the existing "can medical billing and coding be learned online" post.
