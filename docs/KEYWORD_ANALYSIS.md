# Keyword Analysis — medical assistant and medical billing/coding

Source: Emilio's keyword screenshots (Google Keyword Planner, 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-05; Ahrefs keyword ideas; a question tree for "medical coding" and one for "medical assistant"). Analysis by Claude Code, 2026-10-05. The raw screenshots are on Emilio's computer (`~/Desktop/SEO/`), not in the repo.

## How to read the data
- Keyword Planner volumes are rounded to 5,000 / 50,000 / 500,000 and are national. Treat them as tiers, not counts.
- "Competition: Low" in Keyword Planner is **paid-ads** competition, not ranking difficulty. The Ahrefs "KD" (Easy / Medium / Hard) is the ranking signal, and "Easy" still needs links for a new site.
- No Massachusetts-modified volumes yet. Pull "medical assistant massachusetts" and similar terms before relying on local numbers.

## Medical assistant (decided: write first; queue topic target keyword "medical assistant massachusetts")
- Universe: 32,818 phrase-match keywords (medical coding: 15,943; coding and billing: 765).
- **Easy, over 10,000 a month (Ahrefs):** medical assistant jobs, salary, jobs near me, programs. Remote medical assistant jobs: Easy, over 1,000. What is a medical assistant: Medium, over 1,000.
- **Hard (skip as targets):** "medical assistant" (head term), "medical assistant certification."
- **Training/course terms** (about 50,000 in Keyword Planner: training, certified program, classes, course, accredited programs): dominated by schools selling courses. Do not target.
- **Certification terms** (about 5,000 each): cma medical, aama certification, amca certification, american association of medical assistants. Point readers to the certifying bodies.
- **Specialties** (about 5,000 each): dermatology, ophthalmic, ob-gyn, pediatric medical assistant. Good sections or a follow-up post.
- **Local/hiring** (about 5,000): hiring, hiring near me, part time.
- **Wrong intent, ignore:** anesthesia assistant, physician assistants, emergency medical assistant, lab/laboratory assistant, pathologist's assistant.
- **Question tree:** MA vs CNA (who gets paid more, what can an MA do that a CNA cannot, which is harder to get, do CNAs draw blood); hard to pass; worth it; why people quit; hard to get hired; will AI replace MAs; how fast can I be an MA; how long to train; typical and highest pay.
- **Angle:** honest career guide with an MA vs CNA section; hiring reality for beginners; CMA vs RMA and whether certification is required (AAMA CMA generally needs an accredited program); national pay figure (national credential).
- **Follow-up posts, in order:** medical assistant salary; MA vs CNA; CMA vs RMA; remote medical assistant jobs.

## Medical billing and coding (second)
- Salary terms are the biggest Easy cluster (medical coding salary about 50,000 in Keyword Planner; medical billing salary about 5,000).
- Certification terms (CPC, CCS, AAPC) are Hard. Training and class terms are Hard and course-seller territory.
- CPT code terms (cpt codes, 99214, 77063) are people looking up billing codes, not job seekers. Ignore.
- Question tree fits the honest angle: worth it in 2026, AI replacing coders, hard to get hired, remote jobs, billing vs coding pay.
- Remote medical coding jobs: Easy, over 1,000.
- Working title idea: "Is Medical Billing and Coding Worth It? Pay, Training and the AI Question." Link to the existing "can medical billing and coding be learned online" post.
