About Us

Our Mission

We believe that a well-trained workforce contributes to the economic and social vibrancy of Quincy, the South Shore, and the Greater Boston communities in which our students, instructors, and staff live. To accomplish our mission, we are committed to providing a caring learning environment where a technically rich, hands-on, quality education is delivered by professionals who have worked in the field.

  • Flexible schedules to help you reach your potential
  • Knowledgeable instructors to support you along the way
  • In-demand trade, IT, and medical programs to keep you motivated
  • Structured courses to facilitate your success
  • Ongoing partnership to assist in your career journey
