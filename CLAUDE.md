## What this site is
Career Skills Center (Quincy, MA): online career training in Medical Billing & Coding, IT Support and Skilled Trades (coming soon), plus state-funded employee training for Massachusetts employers (Workforce Training Fund – Express Program: workplace ESOL, clinic billing upskilling).

## Hard rules
- Static site. Inner pages are generated by `tools/build-pages.py`: edit `index.html` (shared chrome) or the `PAGES.append(...)` blocks, then rebuild. Never hand-edit generated pages. No new frameworks without asking.
- Never claim ETPL/WIOA approval, "state-approved," or Express Course Directory listing. Copy is controlled by the flags in `js/site-config.js` (`FUNDING_ETPL_APPROVED`, `EXPRESS_PROVIDER_LISTED`), both `false` until Emilio says otherwise. WIOA content stays archived (see handoff).
- Funding numbers (caps, rates, days) come only from `js/site-config.js`. Never hard-code them in copy.
- Never promise anyone they will be approved for funding. Use "may qualify."
- Never invent statistics, outcomes, testimonials or salary figures. Use a marked placeholder plus a TODO.
- Blog posts are drafts until Emilio approves: add `<!-- DRAFT – facts to verify: ... -->` at the top.
- Don't deploy to the live server without Emilio's explicit OK. Commit locally as restore points.
- Audience reads at roughly an 8th-grade level; many read English as a second language. Keep copy plain.
