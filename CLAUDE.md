## What this site is (guide mode, since Sept 26, 2026)
Career Skills Center (Quincy, MA) has **no courses yet**. The site is an honest **guide to careers and training funding in Massachusetts** and sends people **outward** to the state (MassHire Career Centers, JobQuest, mass.gov). CSC plans to offer training in healthcare, IT and the skilled trades; when real courses launch, the site switches to **course mode** and starts sending people **inward** to CSC. The one real service today is for employers: CSC **provides training** for their staff; eligible employers can be reimbursed through the Workforce Training Fund Express Program, which **the employer applies for directly with the state**. CSC does **not** handle the grant paperwork, apply on the employer's behalf, or take payment for the application (Emilio, 2026-09-28: Massachusetts rules mean a provider shouldn't help with or be paid for the application). CSC is not a listed Express provider and has no course of its own.

## Course-dependent content (IMPORTANT)
- Any copy, CTA, link or block that must change when courses launch gets `<!-- COURSE-DEPENDENT: R-xx -->` in the source and a row in `docs/COURSE_CONTENT_REGISTER.md`. Keep the register current every session.
- Switches live in `js/site-config.js`: `SITE_MODE` ("guide"/"courses") and `COURSES_LIVE`. Course-mode copy must never render while they are off.

## Hard rules
- Static site. Inner pages are generated by `tools/build-pages.py`: edit `index.html` (shared chrome) or the `PAGES.append(...)` blocks, then rebuild. Never hand-edit generated pages. No new frameworks without asking.
- Never claim ETPL/WIOA approval, "state-approved," or Express Course Directory listing. Copy is controlled by the flags in `js/site-config.js` (`FUNDING_ETPL_APPROVED`, `EXPRESS_PROVIDER_LISTED`), both `false` until Emilio says otherwise. WIOA content stays archived (see handoff).
- Funding numbers (caps, rates, days) come only from `js/site-config.js`. Never hard-code them in copy.
- Never promise anyone they will be approved for funding. Use "may qualify."
- Never invent statistics, outcomes, testimonials or salary figures. Use a marked placeholder plus a TODO.
- Blog posts are drafts until Emilio approves: add `<!-- DRAFT – facts to verify: ... -->` at the top.
- Don't deploy to the live server without Emilio's explicit OK. Commit locally as restore points.
- **Pre-deploy check (mandatory):** before any upload to the live server, run `grep -rn -e '\[VERIFY' -e 'DRAFT' --include='*.html' .` on the files being deployed. If anything matches, do not deploy those files; list them in `docs/BUILD_STATUS.md`. Fact decisions live in `docs/VERIFICATION_LOG.md`.
- Audience reads at roughly an 8th-grade level; many read English as a second language. Keep copy plain.

## Program facts policy (IMPORTANT)
The old program pages (`it-support-specialist.html`, `medical-billing-coding.html`, `skilled-trades.html`, `tuition.html`, `our-programs.html`, `programs.html`, and program answers in `faq.html`) contain **placeholder content** and are being replaced by career field guides and 301 redirects (see `docs/GUIDE_MODE_SPEC.md`). Lengths, hours, prices, credentials, formats and exam details there are NOT decided and are NOT a source of truth.
- In the blog, landing pages, emails or any new content, **never state Career Skills Center program details** (length, hours, cost, credential/exam, format, certificate, start dates, VA status).
- The only allowed statement: Career Skills Center **plans to offer** training in healthcare, IT and the skilled trades. Readers can get updates when we launch. (Say "healthcare" / "the medical field," not "Medical Billing & Coding," when describing CSC's plans.)
- Talking about industry credentials in general (for example, what CompTIA Tech+ or AAPC CPC is) is fine, as long as it doesn't imply CSC teaches toward it.
- Publish dates must be real: use the actual publish date, never a backdated one.
- **Never publish testimonials, reviews, quotes or outcome stats unless they come from real, consenting students.** The current plan is `docs/GUIDE_MODE_SPEC.md`; it supersedes `docs/PRE_LAUNCH_SITE_SPEC.md` where they conflict.
